Start/sit and waiver calls priced off the betting markets, for this week. Sister page to the season draft board.

Paste your roster, get the optimal lineup. One player per line — names are matched loosely, so “ceedee lamb” or “D. Lamb” both work. Every player is scored on this week’s market lines, then the best legal lineup is chosen for QB / RB / RB / WR / WR / TE / FLEX / FLEX in half-PPR. FLEX takes RB, WR or TE.

Price from these books re-derives every line from only the sportsbooks you tick — score off DraftKings alone, or drop one you think is soft.

Narrowing to a single book drops any stat that book does not post, so totals fall: Drake Maye reads 9.7 on the full consensus but 2.8 on DraftKings alone, because DK prices his rushing yards and not his passing. That is the filter working, not a missing line — use it to ask “what does this book think”, and leave all books ticked for the best projection. Useful when you distrust one number — a touchdown price is far noisier than a receptions line — or to ask a narrower question, like who starts on receiving volume alone. Excluded lines still show, struck through.

A player whose game has kicked off is locked: his actual points are shown, his slot is marked spent, and the optimizer fills only what is still changeable. The total splits into what is banked and what is still to play.

Every row shows the market lines behind its projection — hover any chip to see each book by name with its own number, and a dotted underline marks the ones the books disagree on.

Two players alone works too: add both and the tab tells you which to start and by how much — or use the H2H tab for a stat-by-stat breakdown. Anything the market hasn’t priced is listed separately rather than silently scored as zero — an unpriced player is a market signal, not a projection of nothing.
