Each column is one sportsbook's own posted lines, scored into fantasy points. A blank cell means that book has not priced the player. Consensus is the median line across every venue that priced each stat, scored the same way — it is not the average of the columns, because venues price different subsets of stats.

A number marked * is partial: that book posted only some of the player's markets, so its total is real but lower than a like-for-like comparison. Hover it to see what is missing. Partial cells are left out of Spread, which is the gap between the highest and lowest US sportsbook that priced the same markets — genuine disagreement, which in practice is small (under a point almost everywhere).

Kalshi and DK sit apart for a reason. Kalshi is the only venue here that posts anytime-touchdown markets, worth roughly six points of equity, so its column runs high against books that price yardage only — and it barely moves when you change the scoring format, because it rarely posts a receptions line. DK is a touchdown-only scrape. Neither is counted in the spread, since a different market set is not a different opinion.
